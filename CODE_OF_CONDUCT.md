# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone participating in this project.

Contributors are expected to communicate professionally, respect differing perspectives and experiences, and focus discussions on the technical and scientific content of the repository. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or other inappropriate behaviour will not be tolerated.

If you experience or observe behaviour that violates these expectations, please contact the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled as appropriately and confidentially as possible.
